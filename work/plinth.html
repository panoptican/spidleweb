PLINTH

A literary journal with a new design language every issue.

Role
Design · Typography · Build
Journal and dates
Literary journal · 2014–19
Scope
Seven issues · Hand-written HTML & CSS

I designed the typography and built every page, reshaping a small column grid for each issue. The contents page announced the edition; reading pages gave prose a measured column and let poetry keep its own arrangement.

Published by the Unwin-Dunraven Literary Ecclesia.
Editing: Garett Strickland
Graphics: Tyann Prentice

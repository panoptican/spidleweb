Conservis · Agtech dashboards 2016–21

Field data, captured in the field.

Conservis makes farm management software. Growers use it to track field activity and analyze yields across an operation. At Foundry I led design as the product suite expanded, with a mobile app for the field and new reporting tools for the farm's financial data.

Growers think field by field.

Every grower we talked to wanted data specific to their own fields and crops, down to yield per acre. Even when they're planning and doing the accounting from the office, they think about the operation field by field. So the dashboard puts a map at its core, and the latest numbers always connect to a real place.

The work order comes to the field.

If the essential data on a farm comes from the field, capture it there. On the tablet, a worker can select a task from the work order and see its equipment, inputs, and instructions beside the field map. The task list keeps completed work and work still waiting in view.

The phone carries the same field context into harvest. A grower can open a field and start a transport ticket there. The ticket groups the origin, truck, and destination before the cargo details, keeping the load tied to the place it came from.

The numbers stay tied to the fields.

Every farm is different, so every dashboard is personalized. Growers rotate crops and shift focus through the season and from year to year, and the dashboard's widgets have to keep up. At handoff we left the Conservis team the documentation to build new widgets themselves.

A budget a grower can take to the bank.

Even the most successful growers apply for loans regularly, which means translating handshake land agreements and bushels per acre into the kind of financial data a banker needs. The custom budget reports pull from everything in a grower's account, fertilizer costs to commodity prices, and bundle it into a print-ready PDF a grower can hand to a lender.

The budget starts with a crop plan, assigning fields and acres before working through expenses and land costs. The final report shows totals and costs per acre beside each crop's yield, price, and margin. A grower can review the operation as a whole and still see what each crop contributes.

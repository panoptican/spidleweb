Rate 25 design variants

You'll see 25 variants of the same portfolio index page, one at a time. Scroll to see the whole design.

A rating slider hides in the circle at the bottom of the screen — move your cursor near it (or tap it) to open it. Drag the slider to say how you feel about each one — cool blue for "not for me", hot orange for "love it". There are no numbers; just go with your gut. Use the ← → arrow keys (or the on-screen arrows) to move between variants.

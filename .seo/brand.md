## Brand voice

**This section is the heart of the file.** Get it specific.

- **Voice tags:** plain-spoken, concrete, editorial, quietly confident, dry — says exactly what the thing is and lets the work carry the claim. Short declaratives. Specific numbers over adjectives ("ten years", "fifty homes across ten states"). Occasional literary turn in personal copy, never in case-study facts.
- **Person/perspective:** First person ("I'm a product designer in Minneapolis"). Case studies lead with the user's problem, not the designer's process theater.
- **Forbidden words/phrases:** seamlessly, revolutionary, synergy, leverage (as a verb), delight / delightful, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, passionate, rockstar / ninja, guru, "solutions", "user-centric", "pixel-perfect", "thought leader", "in today's fast-paced world"
- **Forbidden claims:** never claim employment or credit beyond the roster (Blueshift, Vault AI Systems, Foundry); never claim client results or metrics not stated on the case page; never imply availability terms beyond "open to contract and full-time roles"; never state a rate; never claim a degree or certification.
- **Reference brands for tone:** the site's own copy is the reference — GOV.UK's plainness, Stripe's concreteness, and a literary-journal register in personal asides (Jason ran PLINTH).
- **Existing content to match:** `index.html` Profile section and `work/conservis.html` — rhythm, sentence length, and how case studies open with the client's problem.

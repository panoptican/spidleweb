I'm a product designer in Minneapolis. I've spent ten years shipping enterprise software across financial services, agtech, and healthcare: dashboards, data-heavy workflows, and the design systems behind them. I work in Figma every day, from discovery and user flows through high-fidelity UI and developer handoff. I also build working prototypes with coding agents.

Before design, I spent a decade programming and analyzing market research studies. That's where the comfort with dense data started. Most of what I know came from experience: fifty-some homes, ten states, two countries, and about every collar they've fitted for the working class.

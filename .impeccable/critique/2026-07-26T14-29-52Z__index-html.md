#### Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Not AI slop. GT America Expanded, paper/ink/red, clip-path panel, ledger index rows, and per-row hover accents read as an authored system. Soft tells only: em dashes in copy, and the 2×2 Shipped sites grid as the most template-adjacent block. Do not punish the intentional brutalist-editorial archive register.

**Deterministic scan**: 2 findings on `index.html`:
1. `all-caps-body` (warning) — uppercase on ~32 chars of body/footer text (`© 2024 JASON SPIDLE · LINKEDIN ↗`)
2. `design-system-color` (advisory) — `rgb(217, 217, 213)` / `#d9d9d5` flagged as outside DESIGN.md

**Agreement / miss / false positive**:
- LLM and detector agree the page is mostly clean of absolute bans (no side-stripes, gradient text, glass, hero metrics).
- Detector caught `all-caps-body` on the footer; design review did not elevate it (likely intentional chrome). Treat as soft / possible false positive for short branded meta.
- `design-system-color` is a **false positive**: DESIGN.md explicitly lists `site-card-frame.backgroundColor: "#d9d9d5"`.

**Visual overlays**: Mutation + `detect.js` injection succeeded during Assessment B. Overlay labeled `all-caps body text` on the footer. Live server was stopped after evidence capture; overlays may no longer be live in the browser tab.

#### Overall Impression

The homepage already feels like a proof of craft: identity-first panel, ledger as interface, honest density. The biggest opportunity is conversion for a hiring-manager scan: put recent flagship work first, keep UI evidence on mobile, and stop letting Shipped sites compete with Selected Work for the "what does he design?" answer.

#### What's Working

1. **Identity commitment** — Panel, type, paper/ink/red, and ledger rows feel authored. Brand test passes: remove nav and it still reads as this person.
2. **Index as interface** — `.index__row` (thumb · title/kind · year) + hover inversion with per-case `--row-accent` delivers the tactile craft the north star promises.
3. **Honest IA bones** — Selected Work → Sites → Profile → Contact is scannable; micro labels and hairlines reinforce "document" without decoration noise.

#### Priority Issues

**[P1] Oldest-first Selected Work**
- **What**: Index order is Conservis 2019 → Vidscrip 2021 → Expert Insights / Campaign Sim 2025.
- **Why it matters**: Hiring managers have seconds; 2019 farm management leads, recent dense enterprise work sits third/fourth.
- **Fix**: Newest-first, or pin 1–2 flagship 2025 cases; keep ledger chrome.
- **Suggested command**: `impeccable clarify` (IA/copy ordering) or `impeccable layout`

**[P1] Mobile contact email wraps poorly**
- **What**: At ~390px, `.contact__mail` (~22px expanded caps) wraps `jason@spidleweb.net` across two lines.
- **Why it matters**: Peak-end fails on mobile; the ask looks unfinished.
- **Fix**: Smaller fluid size, break at `@`, or stacked "Email" + address treatment.
- **Suggested command**: `impeccable adapt`

**[P2] Em dashes in copy**
- **What**: Profile: "studies — all dense data." Also `og:title`: "Jason Spidle — Product Designer."
- **Why it matters**: Absolute copy ban; default AI punctuation in an otherwise precise voice.
- **Fix**: Period, colon, or rewrite ("…studies. All dense data.").
- **Suggested command**: `impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Sites grid dilutes and competes**
- **What**: Four identical framed `.site-card`s sit as a peer section to Selected Work.
- **Why it matters**: Closest ban-adjacent pattern; marketing/agency screenshots muddy "data-dense enterprise"; second 4-choice wall.
- **Fix**: Demote (compact list, fewer featured, or under Profile), or label harder as "marketing sites, not product case studies."
- **Suggested command**: `impeccable distill` or `impeccable quieter`

**[P2] Mobile strips craft previews**
- **What**: `@media (max-width: 768px)` sets `.index__thumb { display: none }`.
- **Why it matters**: Mobile viewers only see type + vague kinds; thumbs are the density/UI proof.
- **Fix**: Keep a small thumb, or a single hero still for the top case.
- **Suggested command**: `impeccable adapt`

#### Minor Observations

- Accent red is well rationed (nav active, selection, contact hover).
- Empty `alt=""` on index thumbs is OK (title in link text); site cards have real alts.
- `font-display: block` can flash blank type on slow loads.
- Dual labels ("Selected Work" / "Index", "Shipped sites" / "External") are on-register but slightly redundant.
- Sticky panel clip-path is distinctive; watch text vs cut edge at mid widths.
- Footer all-caps is intentional chrome; detector warning is soft.
- Absolute bans mostly clean.

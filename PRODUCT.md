# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Design hiring managers, recruiters, and product leaders looking to evaluate senior product design expertise for data-dense/complex enterprise software. They are often scanning quickly to assess depth of craft, system coherence, and real-world implementation.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio demonstrating Jason Spidle's 10+ years of design lead experience in dashboards, complex workflows, and design systems. The site itself must act as a proof of capability: highly ordered, progressive, performant, and visually distinct.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, Precise, Brutalist.
- **Editorial**: High-contrast, publication-grade typography, paper-and-ink look.
- **Precise**: Hairline rules, mono micro-labels, rigorous grid alignments.
- **Brutalist**: Raw layout structure, bold display typefaces, solid color blocks, and interactive inversions.

## Anti-references

- SaaS templates: neon text gradients, glassmorphism, soft shadows, rounded mockups.
- Bland corporate portfolio templates: generic grids, icon boxes, lazy cards.
- Overly decorated animations: distracting transitions that get in the way of content access.

## Design Principles

- **Archive as Interface**: Frame the content as a raw, documentarian archive. Use micro-labels, registration lines, and numbered rows to denote structure.
- **Density is Clarity**: Respect the user's intelligence by offering clear, structured density instead of sparse, watered-down space.
- **Tactile Progression**: Highlight interactions with sharp physical inversions (e.g., black-to-white or gray-to-color on hover) rather than floaty, bouncy movement.
- **Resilient Static Build**: Zero build tools, plain HTML, vanilla CSS, and progressive JS. The content must load immediately and function perfectly under any conditions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- High text-to-background contrast (ink `#0A0A0A` on paper `#F2F2EF`).
- Keyboard-accessible interactive rows.
- Absolute respect for `prefers-reduced-motion` (reveal animations bypass completely if preferred).
